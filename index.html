🏛️ University Smart Library Regulations

Official operational policies and borrowing standards approved by the Academic Senate.

  • §3.1 Borrowing Quota: Maximum of 3 books concurrently borrowed per registered student.
  • §3.2 Loan Period: Standard duration of 14 calendar days from the date of checkout.
  • §3.3 Overdue Fines: Automatic penalty of ₹2.00 per overdue day per book.
  • §4.1 Online Reservation: Reserved books must be collected within 48 hours from the physical circulation desk.
  • §5.2 Noise Standards: Strict silence maintained in Zone S (Silent Focus Hall); collaborative discussions permitted only in Zone C (Tech Pods).
