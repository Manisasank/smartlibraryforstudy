1. Acceptable Use & Code of Conduct

Use the Smart Library platform exclusively for legitimate academic and educational circulation activities. Unauthorized attempts to alter circulation records, bypass authentication, probe system endpoints, or inject malicious payloads are strictly prohibited and subject to institutional discipline.

2. Account Credentials & Role Integrity

Accounts are assigned designated roles: Administrator, Staff, or Student. Users are legally responsible for safeguarding their login credentials and using only the capabilities authorized for their assigned role.

3. Transparent Circulation & Fee Policies

All overdue fines, book replacement values, and loan durations are governed transparently by institutional academic regulations. There are zero hidden fees, zero surprise surcharges, and zero administrative markups.

4. Limitation of Liability

Smart Library provides digital catalog services on an "as-is" and "as-available" basis for academic support. Scheduled maintenance and system updates are communicated in advance.

1. 100% Fee Transparency & No Hidden Charges

Smart Library does not charge any subscription, registration, or platform access fees. Access to the digital catalog, borrowing privileges, and circulation history is completely free to enrolled members.

2. Overdue Fines & Lost Book Fees

All late return penalties or lost item charges are calculated strictly based on clear per-day institutional rates. Staff and students can view a complete breakdown of any outstanding balance directly on their Fines Statement.

3. Refund & Adjustment Eligibility

• Returned Damaged/Lost Books: If an item marked lost is located and returned in good condition within 14 days, the replacement penalty is refunded or credited immediately.
• Administrative Erroneous Charges: Any discrepancy reported to library staff is audited and rectified within 48 hours.
• Deposit Returns: Institutional library caution deposits are refunded in full upon graduation or formal clearance.

4. Refund Inquiries

To request an audit or refund of an erroneous circulation charge, contact: manisasankreddy@gmail.com

1. Data Minimization (GDPR Art. 5)

Smart Library collects only data strictly necessary for legitimate library management: student/staff name, academic email, institutional role, book requests, active loans, and fine ledgers. We do not collect geolocation, tracking cookies, or biometric data.

2. Use of Information

Information is used solely to authenticate users, manage circulation, calculate overdue fees, provide audit receipts, and fulfill institutional governance requirements. We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

3. Children & Minor Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Art. 8)

Smart Library does not knowingly collect personal data from minors under 16 without authorized school or guardian enrollment consent. Library privileges for minors are administered through institutional education programs.

4. Third-Party SDK & Infrastructure Audit

• Zero Ad Trackers: No third-party marketing, analytics, or behavioral tracking SDKs are deployed.
• Security Architecture: Passwords are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2/SHA256, and sessions enforce CSRF tokens and HttpOnly flags.
• Local Asset Hosting: UI icons and stylesheets are served directly from institutional static storage.

5. Email & Notification Preferences (Unsubscribe)

Automated circulation emails (e.g. overdue notices or hold ready notifications) are transactional and non-marketing. To manage receipt preferences or unsubscribe from non-critical email digests, contact manisasankreddy@gmail.com.

6. Data Deletion & Contact

To request permanent deletion of your profile and circulation records, visit our Data Deletion Request Portal or contact Lead Controller Y Mani Sasank Reddy (manisasankreddy@gmail.com).
